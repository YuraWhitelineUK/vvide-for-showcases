# Video Prompts — "The Season of Decision"

**31 videos total** (1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + 16) across 5 levels.

A wheat farmer named Anna faces crop disease and must make critical decisions.
Each video is **vertical 9:16, 15-20 seconds, cinematic agricultural style**.

**Character consistency for ALL videos:** "Woman in her 30s, dark hair tied back, practical farm clothing — green vest over flannel shirt, jeans, muddy boots. Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, cinematic quality, realistic style, natural lighting, no text overlays, no UI elements."

**Decision videos (L1-L4):** Must end on a clear freeze-frame decision moment.
**Ending videos (L5):** Should feel like a satisfying conclusion.

**Naming:** `Level-Path` where A = primary choice, B = secondary choice.

## PROMPT TIPS FOR GROK

1. **Append to EVERY prompt:** "Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, cinematic quality, realistic style, natural lighting, no text overlays, no UI elements, no words on screen"
2. **Character:** "Woman in her 30s, dark hair tied back, practical farm clothing — green vest over flannel shirt, jeans, muddy boots"
3. **Duration:** 15-20 seconds each
4. **Decision videos (L1-L4):** Must end with a clear freeze-frame moment where the character is visibly paused between two options
5. **Ending videos (L5):** Should feel like a satisfying conclusion — either triumphant or reflective
6. **Consistency:** Keep the same field, same barn, same truck across all videos for visual continuity
